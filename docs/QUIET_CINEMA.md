# Quiet Cinema

Quiet Cinema is Reachmade's default visual direction across the portfolio and product surfaces.

## Core rule

**Keep the screen quiet. Make the product vivid.** Do not add richness by adding decoration. Use composition, a real product surface, restrained motion, readable type and careful spacing.

## Defaults

- Paper: `#F6F5F0`
- Ink: `#20251F`
- Product stage: `#171A17`
- Default accent: `#A83B2F` when a product has no approved accent of its own
- Controls: 8px radius
- Ordinary surfaces: 12px radius
- Product/media stage: 16px radius
- Body copy: normally 16–18px; never make important explanatory text tiny or low contrast

Approved product identity may keep its own accent. Quiet Cinema is not a requirement to recolor every brand red.

## One signature

Choose one implemented moment that explains the product's value and give it the strongest visual emphasis. Examples: a request becoming an artifact, a conversation becoming a saved task, a spoken claim becoming verified evidence, alerts becoming one investigation, a review becoming a revised deliverable, or one recording becoming a launch kit.

Do not make every product use the same UI composition. Share the clarity standard, not a template.

## Avoid by default

- blue/purple AI gradients
- decorative glowing orbs, particles or generic 3D shapes
- full-screen glassmorphism
- grids of interchangeable cards when one product surface can explain the value
- scroll-triggered motion on every element
- vague copy such as “transform the future with AI”
- concept footage presented as if it were a shipped feature

An orb that is a real functional control or state indicator is not prohibited merely because it is circular; decorative AI symbolism is.

## Video and concept states

A product film may be designed before the final UI when that helps define the experience. A scene that is not implemented must be labelled as a **design preview**. Product recordings, scripted demonstrations and conceptual motion must not be presented as the same kind of evidence.

## Release gate

Before treating a Quiet Cinema implementation as done, check desktop and mobile widths, keyboard focus, contrast, reduced-motion behavior, media failure states and the actual implemented product path. A visually polished concept is not a substitute for the real workflow.
